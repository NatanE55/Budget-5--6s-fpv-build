# Bill of Materials (BOM)

This document details the complete hardware list required to reproduce this 5-inch 6S freestyle FPV build. Prices are estimates in USD based on global direct-to-consumer suppliers and may fluctuate.

| Category | Component Description | Technical Specifications | Qty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Frame** | MARK5 225mm Carbon Fiber | 5" Freestyle Geometry | 1 |
| **Flight Controller** | F405 Flight Controller | Standalone FC, 30.5x30.5mm mounting, 5V/9V BEC | 1 |
| **ESC** | 60A 4-in-1 BLHeli_S ESC | 3-6S LiPo input, DShot600 | 1 |
